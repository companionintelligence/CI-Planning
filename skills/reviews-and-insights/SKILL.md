# Reviews and insights

Use `daily_brief` for the current plan summary, `review_sweep` and `possible_tasks_list` for items awaiting triage, and `possible_task_review` to accept or dismiss a candidate. Explain the candidate before acting if acceptance would create or alter a task.

Use `reflections`, `day_stars`, and `day_types` for reflection and day-level records. Use `plan_week` to prepare a weekly plan, and `metrics_read` for measured trends. Describe the metric's date range and meaning; do not present estimates as recorded facts. Confirm before resetting learning data or applying a broad weekly plan. Verify updates with a read tool where possible.
