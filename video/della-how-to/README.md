# Companion Planning · Della Bradley How-To

Eight Lab Films short deliverables in landscape. The picture is the app UI only; Lex is pre-recorded from the saved local profile, and the kit's narrator is not used to replace it.

- Source: the Della chapter captures staged under `assets/video/`.
- Custom full-screen clips: `assets/video/cuts/`.
- Voice: `assets/audio/`, recorded as `lex-local` in `assets/audio/narration.json`.
- Standalone deliverables: `della-howto-01` through `della-howto-08`.
- The implicit film is the opening chapter; the eight short cuts chain to the longer tutorial.

Render with `ci-video build`, `ci-video check`, and `ci-video render --format landscape`.
