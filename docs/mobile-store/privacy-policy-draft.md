## What the app is

Companion Planning is a planning app. It is a client for a Companion Planning server that you run yourself, or that someone else runs for you. The app shows that server's planner, so it does nothing useful without a server.

## What the app stores on your device

- **The server address** you entered on first run, so that the app can find your server.
- **Your session.** After you sign in, the server gives the app a session cookie. The app keeps it in the device's own cookie store.
- **Changes made while offline.** Edits you make without a connection are kept on the device until they can be sent to your server.
- **Cached data**, so that the app can open quickly.

Deleting the app removes this data from the device. Signing out removes the session. Changing the server removes the server address and any changes that have not yet been sent.

## What the app sends, and to whom

The app sends what you enter, and the requests it needs to work, only to the server address you entered. The app contacts no other service. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites. Its font ships inside the app.

## What your server does

Your server may connect to other services if its operator turns them on, for example Google Calendar. Those connections are governed by the server operator's settings and by the other service's own terms, not by this app.
