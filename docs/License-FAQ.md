# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Planning's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Planning source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Planning is a local-first todo and planning app.

Companion Planning uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Planning user?

If you use Companion Planning for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Planning?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Planning under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Planning?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Planning?

No. Selling Companion Planning, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?

Email support@companionintelligence.com.
